# Wave FX11: four end-to-end tests that failed on main after wave FX10

Builder report. It covers what was looked at, changed and proven.

## What was looked at

Main `e005d8a` (wave FX10, a change to the server's search) failed the e2e job of GitHub CI in four tests; `ebf1c66` before it passed. FX10 changed no web code and no test, so the question per test was whether it is flaky (timing, order, shared data) or a real fault.

GitHub keeps no pictures of the run: the e2e job uploads no test results, and its log needs a login. What it does show without one are the annotations: the four errors, with their lines. The CI job runs the same `scripts/e2e` as the build VM, with the same Playwright configuration (one worker, no retries, files in name order), but the whole suite on a faster machine.

On the build VM, on `e005d8a` unchanged, the three files (`0-design.spec.ts`, `frame.spec.ts`, `reading.spec.ts`) ran once: only the Theme test failed, in a different place than on CI. Wave SC6 ran `0-design.spec.ts` three more times on the same commit there, all green.

### Theme in the account menu (frame.spec.ts:248), a real fault

- On the VM, after the switch to a phone, a click on Theme opened nothing: in the trace's film the choices never appear, and Theme ends up focused and closed. On CI, at the same step (line 285), the choices were found and measured, and a moment later the point in the middle of Light reached no choice: they had closed in between.
- The trace's snapshot at that click shows the document scrolled sideways by one pixel: Playwright brings an element into view before it clicks it, and that scrolled the page.
- A sub-menu stands on the screen and shuts on any scroll outside its menu, so it does not stay behind while its entry scrolls away (`MenuSub` in `web/src/components/menu.tsx`, wave FX7). A browser hands a scroll to the page a frame after it happened. So the scroll from bringing Theme into view, which happened before the click opened the choices, reached the page just after, and shut them. Whether it lands before or after the choices were seen depends only on timing, which is why it failed in two places and passed in 30 probe runs.
- A person meets the same: anything that scrolls the page just before a sub-menu opens, or scrolls an area that does not move the entry at all, shuts the sub-menu.

### Shortcuts (reading.spec.ts:704) and the two pictures, not reproduced

- `shortcuts work, never fire while typing, and can be turned off`: after `s`, the message "Added to your favourites." did not appear. It failed on CI twice before (`e4fee88`, `b81b17c`, 2026-11-21) and passed in every run on the VM. A probe with the same steps, with the processor slowed one, four and eight times, logged each key, the focus, the open dialogs and the calls: every time `s` reached the page with the focus on the page and no dialog open, the favourite was stored (204) and the message showed. No cause was found.
- `name-role.png` (3,698 pixels) and `admin-users-1150-light.png` (10,453 pixels): both passed on the VM in four runs of `e005d8a` and in both runs of this wave. Without the CI pictures it cannot be seen what differed, so no picture was retaken and no test was changed.

## What changed

- `web/src/components/menu.tsx`: a sub-menu remembers where its entry stood when it was placed, and a scroll shuts it only when the entry now stands elsewhere (`entryMoved`). A scroll from before it opened, or of an area that does not carry the entry, leaves it open; a scroll that moves the entry still shuts it.
- `web/src/components/the-page.test.ts`: `entryMoved` says no for an entry in the same place, yes for one moved down or one pixel sideways.
- `web/e2e/frame.spec.ts`: a new test opens Theme on a phone, sends a scroll that moves nothing, and requires the choices to stay on the screen and reachable. Before the fix it failed (the choices shut); after it, it passes.

## Decisions

Recorded on the product owner's behalf; they may be overruled.

- **Decided:** fix the sub-menu, not the test. Waiting in the test for the scroll to settle would hide a fault a person can meet.
- **Decided:** the menu of the sidebar that stands on the screen (`Menu` with `floating`) has the same rule, shut on any scroll, but no test or report shows it failing; it is left as it is.
- **Open:** the CI e2e job could upload `web/test-results` when it fails, so a picture that differs only there can be looked at. That needs a new action, pinned by its digest like the others; not done here.
