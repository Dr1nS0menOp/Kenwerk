## 3. What was built

- **`internal/document`.** `searchedAttrs` names, per type of node, the attribute that holds the node's own words (`own`, read inside its block's line, as before) and the attributes shown on a block outside its content (`line`, written by `Text` on a line of their own where the block starts). `wordsOf` reads `own` from it, so the old inline map is gone. The text of existing images and collapsible sections is the same as before; only the five new attributes add lines.
- **The guard.** `TestEveryTextAttributeIsSearchedOrNamedAsNotRead` parses the package's own source with `go/parser`, maps each entry of `attrCleaners` to its function by name, and collects every literal key the cleaner and the functions it calls read with `keepText`, `stringAttr` or `in["key"].(string)`. Each must be in `searchedAttrs` or in `notReaderText` with a reason; neither table may name an attribute the gate no longer keeps. `TestTheGuardSeesTheReadsOfCalledFunctions` proves the walk through called functions (a table's `block_id` is read in `keepBlockID`). Choices (`choice.keep`) are closed lists, not text, and are not collected.
- **`internal/search` (`text_pass.go`).** `TextVersion = 2`; `EntriesAfter`; `Refresh` and `RefreshWorking`, one `UPDATE … FROM unnest($1::uuid[], $2::text[]) … WHERE d.body IS DISTINCT FROM n.body` per batch; the state functions.
- **`internal/pages` (`search_text.go`).** `RefreshSearchText`: one transaction per batch with a statement time of 3 s; one query reads the current version's document of every page in the batch and, where `page_control.head_version > current_version`, the head's; the cursor moves in the same transaction.
- **`internal/app` (`search_text.go`).** The task `refresh-search-text`, every minute; budget 2 minutes, batch 50, a rest 19 times as long as each batch (section 6); waits for older releases and restarts a started pass after one; at the end `FinishTextPass` and the audit event in one transaction.
- **Migration 0080.** `search_state`, one row; starts at version 2 when there are no entries (a new installation), at 1 otherwise.
- **Web.** `offeredKinds(capabilities)` in `search-filters.ts`; `search-filter-bar.tsx` reads the capabilities.
- **Docs.** Spec 087 (status, what moved, "Through the API", D9 to D11), spec 080's SR3 lines, spec 011 D26, SPC-117 in `02-spaces.md`, `docs/operations/upgrade.md` ("Search entries after an upgrade"), `CHANGELOG.md`.

## 6. Load

**Requests.** No query was added to any request; the query budgets of `query_budget_test.go` are unchanged and passed in `make check`. The new words make bodies longer only by the words of captions, titles and buttons.

**The pass, on generated pages** (`TestTheTextPassOnGeneratedPages`, `KENWERK_TEXT_PASS_PAGES=20000`, on the shared build machine; every page about two kilobytes of text and a table with a caption, every entry written with the old text). The real-data run could not be made (section 7). Three runs:

| Run | Batch, rest | Batches | Median batch | Slowest | Work | Whole pass | Reading p95, alone / beside | Saving p95, alone / beside |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1, as planned | 100, once the batch | 201 | 114.4 ms | 384.6 ms | 22.9 s | about 46 s | 19.5 / 29.1 ms | 15.3 / 21.6 ms |
| 2 | 50, 19 times the batch | 401 | 68.3 ms | 164.0 ms | 27.0 s | about 9 min | 19.8 / 28.0 ms | 11.3 / 14.2 ms |
| 3, 200 samples, alone before and after | 50, 19 times the batch | 401 | 46.4 ms | 120.8 ms | 21.2 s | about 7 min | 14.3 before, 14.2 beside, 16.9 after | 6.8 before, 7.2 beside, 8.0 after |

- Entries read 20,002, rewritten 20,001 (the one left was already current), in every run; WAL written by the pass and the work beside it 37 to 46 MB, about 2 kB per entry rewritten.
- Run 1 missed the budget of 10 % clearly, so the pass was made gentler (spec 087, D12). Run 2, with 40 requests per percentile, still showed a rise, but its baselines differed from run 1's by a third (saving 15.3 against 11.3 ms alone), which is the noise of a shared machine, not the pass. Run 3 took 200 requests per percentile and measured alone before and after the pass: beside the pass, reading was as fast as alone before it and saving 6 % slower, both under the slower of the two runs alone. The test now holds the budget against that slower value.
- At this rate a pass of the seeded data's 60,385 pages would take about 20 minutes of wall time, in runs of two minutes each, one after the other; on a quiet machine it is faster.
- Nothing here says anything about 5,000 people (ADR-036): it is one machine, generated pages and two kinds of request.

## 7. Not proven, open

- **The real-data run.** No dump of the seeded data was on the build VM or this machine, so `TestTheTextPassOnRealData` did not run. It is in place (`REALDATA_PACKAGE=./internal/app/ REALDATA_RUN=TextPassOnRealData bash scripts/stress/real-data.sh <dump>`) and must run before the pass is called measured on real data; nothing is claimed for 5,000 people (ADR-036). The stand-in on generated pages is in section 6.
- **The stress profile beside the pass.** The spec asks for the p95 of saving and reading in the stress profile with and without the pass. The tests measure the p95 of reading a page and saving a draft through the whole application, alone and beside the pass, which is the same comparison on one machine without the stress tool; the stress profile itself was not run.
- **Search timings on real data** (`TestSearchOnRealData`) were not run for the same reason. No query of a search changed in this wave; the bodies of pages with captions or titles grow by those words only.
- **A damaged stored document** stops the pass at its batch, with an error in the log at each run (spec 087, "Seen, not changed"). No such document is known; the gate normalizes every document on the way in.
- **`internal/permissions` under `-race`** ran past its 45 minutes on the loaded build machine in the `make check` runs of this wave, a known limit of that machine; it passes on GitHub CI.
