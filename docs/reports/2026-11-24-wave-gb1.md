# Wave GB1: the image of the PDF helper

The helper for PDF files and office previews (Gotenberg) reads files that people upload, so its image matters as much as the app's. Until this wave it ran the upstream image as published, and CI did not scan it. This wave gives it an image of Kenwerk's own, scanned in CI like the other images, tried against the real helper on every change, and published with each release.

## What upstream ships

- Gotenberg builds its images from a fixed Debian snapshot. Both 8.40.0 and 9.9.3 (pushed 2026-11-22) carry the Debian 13 packages of the snapshot of 2026-11-04, Chromium among them, so a published image ages until the next release that moves the snapshot.
- Gotenberg 8.40.0 and 9.0.0 were both pushed on 2026-11-05; no 8.x release followed. Version 9 had ten releases in the next 17 days (9.9.3 on 2026-11-22). Licence MIT; an organisation account, whose releases one maintainer publishes, with several sponsors and about 13,700 stars; OpenSSF Scorecard 5.5, as in the dependency review of 2026-11-24. One active maintainer is below the base rule of two; it was already so for 8.x, and no maintained alternative with Chromium and LibreOffice behind one HTTP API was found.
- Gotenberg 9 drops Java, Python, pdftk, QPDF, pdfcpu, ExifTool and unoconverter from the image, turns macros off by default, and adds deny lists and address filters for LibreOffice and the PDF stages. It removes the Prometheus module, and with it the flag `--prometheus-disable-collect` that `compose.yaml` passed.

## The options, measured

Each option was built and scanned with Trivy 0.76.2 under CI's rule (`--severity HIGH,CRITICAL --ignore-unfixed`) on 2026-11-24:

| Option | Packages | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The upstream images, 8.40.0 or 9.9.3, as published | those of upstream's snapshot | they age between upstream releases, so CI's rule cannot hold them by itself |
| a) 8.40.0 with `apt-get upgrade` | 462; 20 upgraded | passes CI's rule; Gotenberg 8 itself gets no more releases |
| b+a) 9.9.3 with `apt-get upgrade` | 447; 7 upgraded, Chromium and Kerberos among them | passes CI's rule; maintained |
| c) a distribution's own image | not measured | Chainguard's registry refused the pull (a paid image); Debian and Ubuntu ship no Gotenberg |
**Chosen: b+a**, our own small image on Gotenberg 9.9.3. It is clean now, it stays on a maintained line, and it carries fewer programs. The upgrade layer is what keeps it clean between upstream releases, and rebuilding is what keeps an image from ageing: an installation that builds it takes the updates of that day, and CI's nightly build and scan shows when the recipe itself needs a new base. A weekly published image was built, then held back from this wave: publishing it is the product owner's decision.

## Kenwerk's calls against Gotenberg 9

Kenwerk calls `/forms/chromium/convert/html` (with `index.html`, `header.html`, `footer.html`, images, and the fields `paperWidth`, `paperHeight`, `landscape`, `printBackground`, `generateDocumentOutline`, `generateTaggedPdf`), `/forms/libreoffice/convert` (one file, `files`), and `/health`, with basic authentication. Gotenberg 9's release notes (renamed `/forms/pdfengines`, LibreOffice options, `waitWindowStatus`, webhooks, named `paperSize`) seemed to touch none of these. The test against the real helper showed otherwise: Gotenberg 9 answers 400 to `generateDocumentOutline` and `generateTaggedPdf`, renamed `nativeBookmarks` and `nativeTaggedPdf`, so every PDF export failed on it until `internal/exchange/pdf.go` sent the new names. Gotenberg 8.40.0 ignores the new names and still makes the PDF (measured), without outline and tags. The numeric `paperWidth` and `paperHeight` still work, and the flag `--prometheus-disable-collect` had to go.

The lesson for the next major: read the release notes, but trust only a run against the real image. That run is now in CI.

## Found on the way: Kenwerk's ODT export does not open in LibreOffice

The first version of the real-helper test also previewed Kenwerk's own OpenDocument export. LibreOffice refused it on both 8.40.0 ("uno exception") and 9.9.3 ("loadComponentFromURL returned an empty reference"), while the Word export of the same pages opened on both. So the `.odt` that Kenwerk writes is not one that LibreOffice reads; it is older than this wave and has nothing to do with the image. The test now previews the Word export and an RTF file. The ODT writer needs its own look, with this test as the check.

## Gates

All on this PC, in the wave's own Compose project, taken down afterwards.

- **Trivy on the built image** (`docker build deploy/pdf`, CI's rule): passes, no fixable HIGH or CRITICAL.
- **The real helper**, run read-only, without capabilities and with a password, as `compose.yaml` runs it: `TestTheRealPDFHelperMakesAnExportAndOpensOfficeFiles` passes against the new image and against the old 8.40.0 setup. On the new image: 401 without the password, 400 for `downloadFrom`, 404 for `/forms/pdf/merge`, and 403 for a Chromium URL on a public address, which the old setup converted (it relied on the closed network alone).
- **Stand-in tests** of PDF export and office preview (`go test -run 'PDF|Office|Preview|Helper|Export' ./internal/app ./internal/exchange`): pass.
- **`make check`** (`TEST_SHARDS=2`): passes.
- **Mutations** for the changed paths: 6 of 6 caught, four of them new.
- **End-to-end**: `admin-connections`, `blocks`, `file-details` and `space-settings`, the suites that touch PDF and office files: 80 passed.
- actionlint, shellcheck and hadolint on the new workflow, script and Dockerfile: nothing found. actionlint on `ci.yml` shows only two notes at info level, in the step of `release-dry-run` that compares the two builds, which this wave did not touch.
- **After the rebase onto main**, again: `make lint`; the compose, hardening and stand-in PDF tests; Trivy on the image built again without cache (no finding); the real-helper test against it; and actionlint. All pass. `docs/dependency-inventory.md` was generated again.

`pdf-image.yml` runs at a release tag and by hand only. A weekly schedule was built, then held back: a weekly public image is the product owner's to approve.
