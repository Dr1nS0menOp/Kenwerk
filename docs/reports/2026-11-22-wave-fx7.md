# Wave FX7: four things the product owner saw on 2026-11-22

Builder report. It covers what was looked at, changed and proven.

## 1. The edge of the sidebar showed through open menus

- **Cause.** The sidebar is sticky with `--layer-sticky`, so it is a stacking context of its own, and every menu in it, the space menu and the menus of the tree, stacks inside it. The edge that drags the sidebar is a sibling of the sidebar with the same layer that comes later in the page, so it was painted over everything in the sidebar, the menus included, and took the pointer there.
- **Fix.** While the sidebar holds an open menu (`.sidebar:has([role='menu'])`) it takes `--layer-menu`, so its menus lie over the edge. While any menu is open, the edge takes no pointer (`:root:has([role='menu']) .sidebar-edge`): a press on it only closes the menu, as a press anywhere outside a menu does. Both rules use the layer tokens; no new number.
- **Test.** `frame.spec.ts`, "an open menu of the sidebar lies over its edge, and the edge does not drag until it closes": for the space menu and a tree row's menu, the point where the edge crosses the menu reaches the menu, and a drag on the edge beside the menu leaves the width as it was. It failed before the fix with the edge on top.

## 2. Tables had no visible inner lines

- **Cause.** A table in a page had a 1px outline in `--color-guide` and only the line under each row that every table of the app has, in `--color-line` (about 1.3:1 on the page). Between columns there was no line at all, so where cells meet could not be seen.
- **Blueprint.** EDT-159 (built) already asked for "a clear outline and a soft grid". The line now says what the grid is, as the product owner decided on 2026-11-22: a thin line between every two columns and rows, thinner than the outline, at 3:1 on the page in both themes.
- **Fix.** A new token `--color-table-line` (`#7f8b9f` light, `#66748a` dark: 3.4:1 and 3.8:1 on the page, 3.2:1 and 3.6:1 on the quiet fill that wave SC3 gave header cells), added to the contrast pairs of `npm run design` at 3:1 against both. The outline is 2px, the grid 1px, both in that colour, in the reading view and the editor, which share the selectors.
- **Selectors changed** (for wave SC3): only in `web/src/design/document.css`, the rule `.prose .tableWrapper` (its `border`) and one new rule `.prose .tableWrapper th, .prose .tableWrapper td` (`border`). The rule `.prose .tableWrapper table` only gained a comment. Nothing in `blocks.css` or the editor's table controls changed.
- **Test.** `editor-feedback.spec.ts`, "a table has thin lines between its cells inside a thicker outline, for readers and writers": in the reading view and the editor, light and dark, the first heading cell has a line at its end and below it, both thinner than the outline and at 3:1 on what lies under the table.

## 3. The settings button looked like a sun

- **Cause.** `IconSettings` was a small circle with eight rays.
- **Fix.** It is now our own gear on the 24 grid, stroke 1.8: a closed rim of eight teeth around a hub. The settings button is its only user.
- **Test.** `icons.test.tsx`: the icon is two closed shapes, a rim whose corners reach sixteen times to the outer radius (eight teeth) and a hub; the old icon had nine open shapes.

## 4. Theme in the account menu did not show its choices

- **Cause.** Theme had its own sub-menu, positioned absolutely beside the account menu. The account menu scrolls (`overflow-y: auto`), which also cuts off anything that sticks out sideways, so the list was drawn but clipped away; on a phone and right to left it could fall off the window as well. The keyboard test passed because focus and `aria-checked` work on a clipped list: it never checked that the choices could be seen. The design picture `account-menu` showed the same: Theme highlighted and no choices.
- **Fix.** Theme now uses the shared `MenuSub`, as the page menu does. Its list is fixed on the screen and placed by `submenuPlace`, beside the entry on the end side, else the start side, else over the menu, always inside the window. `MenuSub` also opens when a mouse rests on its entry (the focus stays where it is) and closes 300 ms after the pointer leaves, so the pointer can cross the gap; a click on a shown sub-menu moves into it. Theme's own code and its CSS (`.sub-menu`, `.sub-menu-list`, `.menu-arrow`) are gone.
- **Test.** `frame.spec.ts`, "Theme in the account menu opens its choices on the screen by pointer, click and keys": hover, click, Enter, Space and ArrowRight open it, ArrowLeft and Escape close it onto Theme, and on a desktop, a phone and right to left the middle of every choice is inside the window and reaches the choice itself. It failed before the fix at the first hover.

## A test of wave SC3 that raced the editor

On GitHub CI, `links-tables.spec.ts` "the cell with the cursor has a button in its top end corner, and Shift F10 opens its menu" found one cell menu where it expected none. The test clicks the paragraph before the table and presses Shift F10 at once. The key opens the cell menu only while the cursor is in a table, which is right; but the editor takes the new place of the cursor from the browser a moment after the click, so on a slower runner the key still found the cell. The test now waits until the cell button has gone, which happens as soon as the cursor leaves the table. The product is unchanged.
