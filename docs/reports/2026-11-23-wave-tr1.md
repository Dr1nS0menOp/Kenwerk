# Report: wave TR1, the tool rows (spec 100)

The work that waves SC4 and SC5 moved out of their plans: step 10 of spec 081 (ADM-103, D25) and steps 7 and 8 of spec 083 (People's row of tools, the column order and the sort marks, D33 and D34), with the empty blocks of SC4 D24.

## What was studied

Nine lists of the administration and the settings of a space filter today, in seven different ways (the table in spec 100, "Study"). Three facts decided the plan:

- **Two lists filtered in the browser.** Spaces narrowed the whole list it read (`admin-space-filter.ts`), and People narrowed the holders by name. Neither filter reached the API, so a script or an MCP client could not ask the same question, and the product owner's brief asks for server-side filtering only.
- **Two ways to keep filters in the address.** `useAddressFilters` in `views/admin.tsx` kept only the keys it knew and dropped the rest; `useFilters` in `views/content.tsx` kept everything and dropped the page. A section of the settings of a space has `?section=` in its address, so only the second kind works there; it became the one hook, `useListAddress`.
- **Sorting.** `SortTable` sorts in the browser and was the only table with `aria-sort`; Content sorted with a dropdown, Trash with a checkbox, Users not at all. A list that pages (Users, Content) can only sort on the server, so the sort became a parameter with one convention, `sort=<column>` and `-` for the reverse (D7).

What was weighed and left: a free-text search of the Audit log. The only cheap search is the exact action, through `audit_events_action_idx (action, seq)`; anything partial reads the log backwards until it finds 100 rows, a full scan on a large instance (D6). The count line's "out of how many" (ADM-105) is given only for Spaces, which reads its whole list anyway (D4).

## What was built

- `ToolRow` (`components/tool-row.tsx`) with its model (`tool-row-model.ts`), `useListAddress` (`list-address.ts`) and `SortHeading` (`sort-heading.tsx`). A filter is either a menu of radio entries (fixed values) or a small panel with a field (a person, days, a number, a short text), both closing with Escape onto their button.
- The row on Users, the Audit log and Spaces in the administration, and on People, Permissions, Content, Trash, Files and Templates in the settings of a space.
- Sortable headings on Users (name, e-mail, last login), Spaces (name, key, pages, storage, last change), Content (title, owner, created, last change) and Trash (name, deleted); the fixed order of the Audit log and of the Audit of the space is marked on When.
- People: Kind and Role filters, Add people in a dialog that reads the whole list, Export as CSV in the title row's More, the line "Service account" and the Guest mark from the new `user_type`.
- The empty block of NAV-173 on Automation (instance and space), Templates (instance and space) and Document control.
- Server: `sort` on `GET /admin/users`; `q`, `status`, `min_pages`, `min_mib`, `quiet_months`, `sort` and `total` on `GET /admin/spaces`; `q`, `kind`, `role` and `user_type` on both role-assignment lists; `q` on `GET /spaces/{id}/content`; `q` and `state` on both template lists. No route, rule or scope changed.
- Removed: `views/admin-space-filter.ts` and its test, the `FilterSelect` and `useAddressFilters` of `views/admin.tsx`, `useFilters` of `views/content.tsx`, the CSS of `.file-filters` and `.content-filters`, and the translations nothing used any more.

## Tests

- Written first, through the API with its rules (`internal/app/tool_rows_test.go`): the user sort in both directions and a refused column; the spaces filter, sort, `total` and refusals, and a reader refused; People narrowed by kind, role and name, a service account's `user_type`, a role filter that never reaches another space's holders, refused to someone without `space.manage_access` (403) and to a stranger (404), and `400` for an unknown kind or a role that is no id; the Content search that does not find a restricted page with a matching title; the template filter by name and by each state.
- Pure Go tests: `list_params_test.go` (the sort convention, counts, fixed lists) and `admin_spaces_test.go` (every filter, every sort, the totals kept in step).
- `components/tool-row.test.ts`: the words of a set filter, the line of more filters, what Reset clears and keeps (the section of the settings), the address of a change, and every step of sorting.
- `web/e2e/tool-rows.spec.ts`: a filter from its menu reaches the address and the request, survives a reload, Reset clears it; Escape gives the focus back; More filters shows by itself while one of its filters is set; a sorted column in the address with `aria-sort`; the Audit log's export carries the filters; Spaces searched on the server; People by kind, Add people, and a reader refused; the Content search.
- Existing e2e that used the old controls were changed to the new ones only: `people.spec.ts`, `file-details.spec.ts`, `organising.spec.ts`, `access-roles.spec.ts`, `roles.spec.ts`, and `0-design.spec.ts`.
