# Report: wave AR1, rules as a chain of cards (spec 091)

One of the parts before 1.0 the product owner asked for, as an experimental part that is off by default (ADR-043): a rule's own screen with its steps as a chain of cards, a panel beside them, and a check on the server that says while typing what a rule still needs. Spec 091 was studied on 2026-11-16 on a branch of its own; this wave brought it onto today's `main` and built stages 1 to 6 and 8.

## What changed since the study

`main` moved a long way after the study. Spec 091 now has a section "Updated for main on 2026-11-23" that says how, and wins where it differs:

- **RV12 and RV13 (specs 092, 093)** settled who may change a rule and who may start it outside its trigger. The screen keeps every guarantee and shows it: a person who manages a rule but does not hold it sees a note, and saving asks first and then takes the rule over (`take_over: true`); only the holder sees Make an address; switching off needs no take-over; a test run says whose eyes it reads with; conditions under a trigger without a page are a problem of fit with its advice. The rule's answer gained `you_hold` so the screen can say this before a save fails; the server decides either way.
- **SC4 and SC5** gave the frames: a rule of a space opens inside its space's Settings frame through `SpaceToolFrame`, a rule of the instance inside the administration's frame, now reusable as `AdminFrame` (E2).
- **TR1 (spec 100)** gave Automation its empty block, and no tool row, because it is a short list (E3). The block's button opens the new screen's draft while the part is on.
- **MC2 (spec 096)**: both new routes have a scope (E7).
- **QG1, QG2**: no line was added to the lint ratchet (E8).

## Decisions taken on the product owner's behalf (2026-11-23, may be overruled)

E1 to E8 in spec 091. The ones that matter most:

- **E1. The condition after an action (stage 7) waits for a wave of its own.** This answers the open question of D3. It keeps this wave free of a migration and of any change to the format of a rule, so nothing is held during a rolling upgrade. The add button after an action offers Action only.
- **E4. Names on cards come from the server, as the viewer may see them.** The check answers `names`: a page's title only through the page's own access rule (`pages.ChosenPage`), a space's name through its own (`spaces.NameFor`, new and lighter than `Get`), a template through the template module. Anything else reads "a page you cannot see". The browser never asks for a title by itself, and an MCP client gets the same names.
- **E5. No new colour values.** The kinds use existing mark tints, the warning the colours of the warning notice.
- **E6. The value picker is a floating `Menu`** of today's six values; its search and kind filter wait until there are more values.

## What was built

- **Server.** The experimental part `rule_cards` (`internal/features`), on in the capabilities only while Automation is on too. Every problem of a rule now has `where`, `kind` (`input` or `fit`) and `code` besides its `step` and `message`; each item of an `all` group gets its own problem. A rule without a trigger is refused on create, change and take-over. Switching on a rule that is not ready answers `400 rule_not_ready` with every problem as a field. `Service.Check` with `names`, behind `POST /api/v1/spaces/{id}/rules/check` (`object:space.automation`, `content:read`) and `POST /api/v1/admin/rules/check` (administrator, session only, `admin:read`); both answer `404 experimental_off` while either part is off.
- **Browser.** `views/rule-definition.ts` (the rule model and its lists, moved out of `rules.tsx`, so the form and the cards share one list), and `views/rule-chain/`: `chain-model.ts` (pure: cards and back, add, copy, remove, move, where each problem goes, the canonical form that "Changes not saved" compares), `sentences.ts` (pure: every title and second line as one translated sentence), `words.ts`, `chain.tsx` (canvas, cards, add buttons, zoom), `panel.tsx` (summary, details, lists of types, a step's settings), `step-forms.tsx` (every type the model holds, including the five condition kinds and four actions the form left out), `rule-screen.tsx` (frames, header strip, split button, More, saving, the leave guard). Components `SplitButton` and `ZoomControl`; icons for zooming and for the kinds; `design/chain.css`; a leave guard in the router; `useKnownCapabilities`; `useDelayed` takes a pause.
- **Not built** (stays as spec 091 says): stage 7; the arm of PLT-138; the help panel of PLT-160 (help links open the documentation); the description, failure notices and labels of PLT-079 to PLT-084; the template mark after saving (D13).

## Keyboard and screen readers

Every card is a `button` in an ordered list named "Steps of the rule"; its name is "When:", "If:" or "Then:", its title, its line, and its problems in words. Selecting a card moves the focus to the panel's heading; Escape in the panel returns it to the card. Alt with Arrow up or Arrow down moves a card, the focus stays on it, and a live region says "Add a label moved to place 3 of 3". The add button under a placeholder is unavailable and says why. The split button is unavailable until a trigger is chosen and says why. Every change, a value put into a field and a save are said in the live region; removing a step offers Undo in a toast. Nothing needs dragging (WCAG 2.5.7).

## Load

- **Opening a rule** costs what it did, plus one check.
- **A check** runs at most once per 400 milliseconds of typing per person editing a rule. It does the pure judging, the existing lookups of `goneThings` (one per named group, one per outward step), and one lookup per thing the rule names, each through its module's access rule. Measured on the build VM for a rule naming one page and one group: 19 statements, now the budget in `rule_cards_test.go`. A rule names at most about 100 things (50 conditions, 50 actions), so the worst case is a few hundred statements, on a settings screen used by a few people; at 5,000 people this is not measured (nothing is claimed for it).
- **Saving** is the same `POST` or `PUT` as before. No worker job, cache or table is added; nothing on reading or saving pages changes.
