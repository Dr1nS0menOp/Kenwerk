# Handoff: wave SO1, the security overview (spec 088)

**Done.**

- **Administration, Security, Security overview**, an experimental part (`security_overview`) that starts off on every installation, new or upgraded. 16 steps in four groups (sign-in and accounts, tokens and sharing, installation, backups and audit), each done, needing attention or not started, with sub-steps, a count, figures where they help and a link to the section where the step is done. A switch hides the steps that are done (`?done=hidden`). Two cards: the audit log and the documentation.
- The rules are `internal/hardening` (pure, with `Cache`, 30 seconds per node). The facts are read in `internal/app/security_overview.go`, each through its owner's Go API: `identity.SecurityCounts` (new, one statement), `sites.LiveEverywhere` (new), `instance.CreatedAt` (new), `db.CurrentRole` (new), `scim.Tokens`, `exchange.Limits`, `backups.Overview`, `audit.List`, and the cached health facts. `health.backupWarnAfter` became the exported `health.BackupDueWithin`, so the overview and System health judge a backup alike.
- `GET /api/v1/admin/security-overview`, `permission:administrator`, scope `admin:read`, open to tokens and so to assistants; `404 experimental_off` while off. The module checks administration itself as well. The attention list gains `security_steps` while the part is on. Capabilities gain `security_overview`.
- Web: `views/admin-security.tsx`, `views/security-overview-model.ts`, `components/security-step.tsx`, `IconOpenCircle`, styles in `design/layout.css`, the registry entry first in Security, words in `en.json`.
- Docs: spec 088 rewritten as built, `docs/operations/security-overview.md` (new), `upgrade.md`, `README.md`, the design standard's component table, blueprint 03 (ADM-125 to ADM-127 partly, ADM-128 and ADM-191 built), `CHANGELOG.md`.
