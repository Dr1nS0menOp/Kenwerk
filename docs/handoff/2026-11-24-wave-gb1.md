# Handoff: wave GB1, the image of the PDF helper

**Done.**

- The helper for PDF files and office previews runs Kenwerk's own image, `deploy/pdf`: Gotenberg 9.9.3, pinned by digest, with Debian's security updates applied at build time, and Kenwerk's settings in `deploy/pdf/start.sh`. `compose.yaml` builds it (`kenwerk-pdf:local`), or pulls `KENWERK_PDF_IMAGE`; `compose.dev.yaml` builds the same image.
- New locks: Chromium and LibreOffice refuse every address, public or private; macros never run; the routes and stages for changing PDF files are off. `TestThePDFHelperStartsLockedDown` holds the flags and the hardening of the `pdf` service, with four new mutations.
- CI's `container` job builds the image with `--pull`, scans it with the same pinned Trivy rule as the other images, and runs `TestTheRealPDFHelperMakesAnExportAndOpensOfficeFiles` against it: a PDF export on A4 and Letter with contents, cover, header and footer, and the preview of Kenwerk's own Word export and an RTF file. It also checks that the helper refuses a request without its password.
- That test found that Gotenberg 9 refuses two field names of 8; `internal/exchange/pdf.go` now sends `nativeBookmarks` and `nativeTaggedPdf`. Gotenberg 8 ignores them (measured), so a helper of 8 that an operator runs still makes PDF files, without outline and tags.
- `.github/workflows/pdf-image.yml` builds, scans, publishes, signs and attests `ghcr.io/<owner>/kenwerk-pdf` at every release tag, and when run by hand. It has no schedule.
- Docs: `docs/dependencies.md` (new section, Helper images), the inventory rows, `docs/operations/install.md` (keeping the helper current, verifying the published image), `upgrade.md`, `release.md`, blueprint 04 (ARC-085, ARC-090), CHANGELOG.

Report: `docs/reports/2026-11-24-wave-gb1.md`.

**For the product owner to decide.**

- **Gotenberg 9.** A new major of a dependency. Chosen because Gotenberg 8 had its last release on the day 9.0 came out, so 8 gets no fixes of its own any more, while 9 also carries fewer programs. Two field names changed (above). It may be overruled: the image also builds on 8.40.0 by changing its `FROM` line and dropping the flags 8 does not know, and the PDF then loses its outline and tags unless the old names come back.
- **A weekly published image.** The coordinator held the Monday schedule of `pdf-image.yml` back from this wave and asked the product owner by mail: a weekly public image is theirs to approve. Until then the image is published at each release and by hand, and installations build it themselves (`compose.yaml`). Approving it is one line: a `schedule` trigger, `cron: "41 4 * * 1"`, in `pdf-image.yml`. The first publish leaves the package private on ghcr.io; it must be made public, as the other packages were (`docs/operations/release.md`).

**Found, not fixed.** Kenwerk's own `.odt` export does not open in LibreOffice, on Gotenberg 8 or 9 (report). Its writer needs a look of its own.

**Next.** After the merge, run `pdf-image.yml` once by hand, make the package public, and check `cosign verify` as `install.md` says. Gotenberg 9 releases often: Renovate's Monday pull requests move the base, and CI's real-helper test is what proves each one.
