# Handoff: wave SC3, the editor as blueprint 03 arranges it (spec 082)

**Done.** All twelve steps of spec 082, after the product owner's answers of 2026-11-21 (the table button and bar yes, the publish step yes, the insert dialog as a combination with the preview kept in the tooltip):

- **Strip** (`views/edit-strip.tsx`): icon and bold title, Unpublished changes and the public mark at the start; save status, people with Invite, **Publish…** and **Close** at the end; three bands with their lines. Comment left the strip.
- **Publish step** (`PublishStep` in `components/publish-options.tsx`): summary, notify watchers, status, date, Preview and Location; Publish or Schedule and Cancel; Discard draft (or Revert to published) apart, asking first, absent while editing together. `Ctrl Enter` publishes at once. Every e2e spec publishes through `publishFromEditor` in `e2e/helpers.ts`.
- **Row of tools** (`components/editor/toolbar.tsx`, `tool-fold.ts`, `key-names.ts`): the new order, More formatting, List choices, direct buttons, table at once with its arrow for the size, undo and redo dimmed in place, History, Find and Full width last, the accent-on-tint active look, folding in a fixed order.
- **Sheet**: the reduced byline (`views/edit-byline.tsx`), the help button and `Ctrl /` (`components/editor/editor-help.tsx`) with the typing shortcuts in the overview.
- **Catalogue, insert dialog and slash menu** (`insert-catalogue.ts`, `insert-dialog.tsx`, `insert-grid.ts`, `insert-entry.tsx`, `insert-preview.tsx`, `slash-menu.tsx`): categories, roles, shortcut boxes, icon tiles, new entries; the dialog with categories, a three-column grid and the tooltip with a drawn preview (D22); the flat slash menu with its last row. `browse.tsx` is gone.
- **Tables** (`table-cell-menu.tsx`, `table-bar.tsx`, `table-cells.ts`, `table-frame.tsx`, `table.ts`): cell button and menu, Shift F10, empty the cell, cell alignment, the empty-cell hint, the table bar with Table settings and More, select-all, header fill. The one table button is gone.
- **History** (`views/edit-history.tsx`): a panel with the versions, one or two compared, and Restore into the draft with Undo, also editing together.
- **API**: `GET /api/v1/document/format` (new, signed in or a read token) describes the document format from the gate's own lists. Every other route the editor uses already existed; spec 082, "Through the API", lists them.

No migration, no setting, no change to the document format. Report: `docs/reports/2026-11-22-wave-sc3.md`.

**Not built, by decision:** the fold control (D1); the New mark, the gallery and the starter set (D20); `Ctrl Alt` with the arrows (D15); vertical alignment in a cell (D14); switches for sticky headers and the reader filter (D16).
