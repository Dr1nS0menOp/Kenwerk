## Gates

All on the build VM, which ran at a load of 15 to 35 on 10 cores throughout.

- `make check` without the race tests (format, vet, staticcheck, govulncheck 1.10.0, lint with its ratchet, secrets, `npm audit`, licences, web and collab checks): **green** on the final tree.
- The Go tests under the race detector: **not green on the VM, and not tried a fourth time.** Three runs ended in the 45-minute timeout of `internal/app` (and the known timeout of `internal/permissions`), each while an ordinary test was a few seconds old; no run printed a single `--- FAIL`. Run alone in two shards, one shard of `internal/app` passed in 43 minutes and the other timed out. Every other package passed. Without the race detector, `internal/app` and `internal/permissions` also hit 45 minutes with no failure. They are proven on GitHub CI after landing, as the coordinator decided.

## For the product owner to decide

- **How Renovate's updates land.** Renovate runs and finds updates within days, but nothing lands: waves are pushed straight to `main`, nobody merges pull requests, and the DCO check fails on Renovate's commits because they carry no sign-off. Options: (a) let a builder take Renovate's pull requests into a wave every week, as this wave did by hand; (b) exempt the Renovate bot from the DCO check and merge its green patch and digest pull requests; (c) turn on Renovate's automerge for patch updates and digests once checks pass. Until one is chosen, digests and patches fall behind again within weeks, against the rule of 2026-11-23.
