# Handoff: wave AR1, rules as a chain of cards (spec 091)

**Done.**

- A new experimental part, **Rules as a chain of cards** (`rule_cards`), off by default and on in the capabilities only while Automation is on too. With it off, rules are the form of before.
- With it on, a rule opens on its own screen inside the Settings frame of its space or of the administration: the header strip with Save and switch on / Save switched off and More (turn off, copy, export, test, run log, delete), the steps as cards with an add button after each and a zoom, and the panel with the summary and details, the lists of types, or one step's settings. A new rule is a draft until its first save.
- The server is the one judge: `POST /api/v1/spaces/{id}/rules/check` and `POST /api/v1/admin/rules/check` say what a rule still needs (with `where`, `kind`, `code`) and name what it refers to as the caller may see it. A rule without a trigger is refused; switching on a rule that is not ready answers `rule_not_ready`. The rule's answer has `you_hold`.
- The guarantees of RV12 and RV13 hold on the screen: a non-holder's save takes the rule over only after asking, only the holder sees Make an address, conditions without a page are a problem of fit.
- No migration.

Report: `docs/reports/2026-11-23-wave-ar1.md`. Spec: `docs/specs/091-rules-as-a-chain-of-cards.md`, with "Updated for main on 2026-11-23".
