# Handoff: wave OD1, the OpenDocument export

**Done.**

- Kenwerk's `.odt` export opens in LibreOffice again. The cause: the `mimetype` entry was stored with a zip data descriptor, which Go's `CreateHeader` adds and LibreOffice refuses for a stored entry. `internal/office/odt.go` now writes it with `CreateRaw`, sizes and checksum in its header.
- The file claims OpenDocument 1.3, and the manifest's package entry has its version. A panel, columns or table inside a list item is written as its blocks, and empty grids are left out, so `content.xml` matches the OASIS schema.
- Tests: `internal/office/odt_test.go` (package rules, namespaces and version, the OASIS 1.3 schema with `xmllint`, and LibreOffice in the real helper when `KENWERK_TEST_PDF_URL` is set). The ODT export joins the previews of `TestTheRealPDFHelperMakesAnExportAndOpensOfficeFiles`. CI's `go` job installs `libxml2-utils`, and its container job runs both live tests. Seven new mutations in `scripts/mutation-check.sh`.
- The development toolbox (`Dockerfile.dev`) installs `libxml2-utils`; `scripts/dev up` rebuilds it.
- The schemas are in `internal/office/testdata/odf`, unchanged, with OASIS's notice.

Report: `docs/reports/2026-11-24-wave-od1.md`.

**For the product owner to decide.** Nothing.

**Next.** `.odt` files exported before this fix stay unreadable for LibreOffice; exporting again is the remedy (CHANGELOG). Word and other OpenDocument readers were not tried.
