# Handoff: wave MC2, scopes per route and tokens with limits (spec 096)

**Done.**

- Every route names its scope in one table beside the router (`internal/httpapi/scopes.go`); a route a token can reach without one stops the server from starting. `routes.txt` and `openapi.json` print it.
- Thirteen scopes: `content:read`, `content:write`, `content:delete`, `content:share`, `content:export`, `spaces:manage`, `people:read`, `account:read`, `account:write`, `admin:read`, `admin:audit`, `admin:system`, `admin:write`, and `none` for routes no token ever reaches. Sharing out, public links, sites, guests, restrictions, access requests and group members are `content:share`; a space's access, roles, webhooks, automation, deleting it and its trash are `spaces:manage`. The word `write` no longer covers them for new tokens.
- Old tokens keep exactly what they had; an old `write` token that shares or changes access leaves `via.old_write` in the audit log.
- Tokens may be limited to up to 50 spaces, checked in `permissions.Evaluate` and in the perm_* list functions (new signatures with `allowed uuid[]`); outside the limit answers `404`. Without a choice a token reaches every space (the product owner's decision).
- "For an assistant" tokens, with 120 changes and 20 deletes or shares a minute; 1,200 calls a minute for all tokens of a person.
- `via` on every audit event caused through a token.
- The token screen of Your account: scopes in plain words, every space or chosen spaces, For an assistant.
- `docs/api/README.md` now says the API may change radically until 1.0, and only grows from 1.0 on.

Migration: `0078_credential_limits.sql` (expand). Renumber it if another wave lands 0078 first; nothing else refers to the number.
